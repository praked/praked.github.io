<p><strong>Misc</strong></p>

<p>When I'm away from all my electronic devices I need a few things to have a good
time. Apart from my friends, a good view where I can put my photography skills to
use, LEDs and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pizza">pizza</a> top that list.</p>

<p>The first 18 years of my life were spent in a small town called
<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faridabad">Faridabad</a>. Wherever I go, this
town always travels with me and always enriches me.</p>

<p>This is my travelogue, in the form of images from various places I have visited.</p>
